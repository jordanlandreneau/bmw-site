## Service Calls Checklist

**Greet the customer with the dealership name:**

- “It’s a wonderful day here at BMW and MINI of Manhattan. My name is (your name). How can I help you?”

**Pull up the customer's file:**

- “What is your (phone number/Last 8 of VIN/Name/email) so that I can pull up your file here with us?”

Confirm the name of the customer on file and email (If there is no name)

**Confirm the customer's vehicle**

- (Year, Make, and Model) <span class="red">**ASK FOR VIN # always.**</span> If they don’t have it it’s okay but make sure to ask. <span class="red">**This is extremely important for BMW and MINI. We need to schedule customers at the correct dealership.**</span>

**Enter ALL services requested by the customer.**

**Ask for details.**

- Ex. If they want to bring a vehicle in for a flat tire, ask them which tire it is.
- Ex. If they are saying that their car is making a weird noise. Ask questions.
- Is the car making noise when you speed up or slow down, when you go above a certain speed?
- The more information we provide, the better it is for the dealership.

<span class="red">**We want QUALITY appointments.**</span>

- Let the customer know that they will receive a text confirmation shortly and thank them for contacting the dealership.

### ATTENTION!!

- <span class="red">DO NOT GIVE OUT PRICING FOR ANY SERVICE UNLESS ITS PROVIDED TO YOU OR IT IS LISTED ON THE WEBSITE.</span>
- <span class="red">**NO LICENSE PLATE = NO SERVICE PER JAMES** Even if a customer has ownership and title of vehicle, dealership will not take a look at it.</span>

**LATE CUSTOMER**

The answer for all customers wanting to come in late for an appointment should be a blanket “we will accept your vehicle but we can’t guarantee you will be seen immediately by an advisor nor can we promise a turn around time.

**TIME ESTIMATES**

- <span class="red">DO NOT GIVE OUT A TIME ESTIMATE FOR ANY SERVICE- Do not say **“It shouldn't take long” or “It should be a quick service”**</span>
- <span class="red">The answer is always “It depends on your advisor's day and how they are scheduled. however, your service advisor will be able to give you a more accurate time estimate.” Never give out any sort of time estimate!</span>

### Heat Case Handling

- <span class="red">If the customer is using abusive language you can give them one warning afterwards, disconnect the call and alert James</span>
- <span class="red">You can let the customer know that the fastest way for you to help them is getting off the line with them to email the advisor.</span>
- <span class="red">You can let the customer that you are hosted offsite</span>
- <span class="red">You can also provide the email and direct number to each advisor.</span>
