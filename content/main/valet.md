## Valet Information

- If the customer asks “ When will my vehicle be picked up?” <span class="red">There is no set time. Do not promise anything.</span> The vehicle will be picked up any time from 8 am-5 pm. The pickup team will call an hour before pick-up.
- There is no fee for Valet service
- <span class="red">There is never same-day service for valet.</span>
- There will be a 2 day average turn around
- The valet team needs garage authorization
- If it is too dangerous to drive they will not pick up (No drivetrain malfunctions/flat tires) Refer to roadside assistance

**GENERAL GUIDELINES**

- 8AM - 5PM TIME FRAME FOR PICK UP **(MAKE SURE TO TELL CUSTOMERS THIS)**
- THEY WILL CALL ABOUT 1 HOUR PRIOR OF THE PICK UP **(LET CUSTOMER KNOW)**
- **INFORMATION NEEDED TO SET VALET**
    1. FULL ADDRESS INCLUDING ZIP CODE (WHETHER IS A PRIVATE RESIDENCE OR GARAGE ADDRESS)
    2. PARKING SPOT NUMBER (IF VEHICLE IS AT A GARAGE)
    3. LICENSE PLATE NUMBER (IF VEHICLE IS IN PRIVATE RESIDENCE)
    4. GARAGE PHONE NUMBER
    5. CLIENTS PHONE NUMBER
- VALETS ARE DONE MONDAY - FRIDAY ONLY
- HE DRIVER WILL BE WEARING A BLUE POLO THAT SAYS BMW OR PLM
- **HOW TO SCHEDULE A VALET APPT ON MYKAARMA:**
    1. SELECT PICK UP AND DELIVERY
    2. CHOOSE THE CORRECT TIME 5:50 AM FOR BMW AND 6 AM FOR MINI
    3. ADD EVERY SINGLE SERVICE REQUEST
    4. ADD THE PICKUP AND DELIVERY INFORMATION UNDER “REPAIR CONCERNS”
    5. CHOOSE THE 5:50AM FOR BMW AND 6AM FOR MINI
- <span class="red">**ONLY SCHEDULE VALET FOR REGULAR SERVICES**</span>
    - <span class="blue">**Yes to:**</span>
        - OIL CHANGE
        - TIRE ROTATION
        - STATE INSPECTION
        - CAR WASH
        - ANY BASIC WORK
    - <span class="red">**No to:**</span>
        - <span class="red">DIAGNOSIS WORK</span>
        - <span class="red">CHECK ENGINE</span>
        - <span class="red">FLAT TIRES</span>
        - <span class="red">VEHICLE IS RUNNING FUNNY OR NOT CORRECT</span>
        - <span class="red">DEAD BATTERY</span>
        - <span class="red">VEHICLE DOES NOT TURN ON</span>
        - <span class="red">ANYTHING THAT IS NOT SAFE TO BE DRIVEN / DRIVABILITY CONCERN WILL NOT BE PICKED UP</span>
- SOMEONE MUST HAND OFF THE KEY TO THE DRIVER (EX: GARAGE ATTENDANCE, NEIGHBORS, FRIENDS, ETC)
