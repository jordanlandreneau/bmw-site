- **KHFY** - Canberra Beige Perforated SensaTec: A light beige synthetic leather material used in many BMW models, designed for comfort and durability.
- **KHG7** - Tacora Red Perforated SensaTec: A bold red synthetic leather option that provides a sporty and modern interior feel.
- **KHKC** - Cognac Perforated SensaTec: A warm, brownish-orange synthetic leather that adds a touch of luxury to the vehicle's interior.
- **KHMY** - Black Perforated SensaTec: A classic and elegant black synthetic leather, widely used across various BMW models for a clean and timeless look.
- **KHRI** - Cognac Sensafin Sensa: A high-end, vegan-friendly synthetic material in a rich cognac color, offering the feel of real leather.
- **KHSW** - Black Perforated SensaTech: Similar to KHMY, this is another variation of synthetic leather in black, known for its breathability and comfort.
- **KPRI** - Oyster Vernasca Leather: A premium light grayish-beige leather option, known for its soft feel and luxurious appearance.
- **KPSW** - Black Vernasca Leather: A durable and elegant black leather choice that is both luxurious and easy to maintain.
- **LCRO** - Mocha Vernasca Leather: A deep brown leather option that gives the interior a rich and inviting atmosphere.
- **SAMY** - Silverstone Extended Merino Leather: A light gray, almost silver leather option, available in some high-end BMW models, offering a luxurious feel.
- **SCHA** - Tartufo Full Merino Leather: A dark brown, almost chestnut leather, known for its supple texture and premium quality.
- **SCIC** - Fiona Red/Black Extended Merino Leather: A striking combination of red and black leather, providing a sporty yet elegant interior option.
- **VCSW** - Black Full Merino Leather: An all-black premium leather, offering the highest quality and luxurious feel in the BMW lineup.
