> **Information about the Integrated Brake (IB) module (recall)**
>
> - The Integrated Brake (IB) module may not function properly.
> - A warning message will be displayed on the instrument cluster.
> - There may be a reduction in power-assisted braking, increasing stopping distance and crash risk.
> - The ABS and DSC systems may fail, requiring greater pedal force.
>
> **Important**
>
> - Fully mechanical braking is not affected.
> - If the IB module fails, the emergency brake will automatically activate.
> - The emergency brake force distribution system remains available for stability control.
>
> **BMW contact process**
>
> Yes, BMW is in the process of implementing the recall to ensure the availability of necessary parts and tools.
>
> **Additional notes**
>
> - No parts are currently available, and a notification will be sent when they are ready.
> - If warnings appear, take the vehicle to the nearest BMW center.
