1. Customers receive an email 3.5 months before their lease ends reminding them to schedule a final service appointment that they MUST complete prior to turning in their vehicle. If they do not do this they will be charged $700.
2. Once they complete their service, they must then schedule a final inspection which gets scheduled through Auto-VIN: 1800-556-2811 Give the customer that number, they are in charge of scheduling that themselves. <span class="blue">(They will receive a final report from their inspection that is NEEDED in order to turn in their lease)</span>
3. Once they have completed their final service AND got the final inspection papers then they can finally turn the car in. End of lease returns goes to Jodi (Send info to Lauren and Coral) - Adriana Contreras: Adrianna.Contreras@bmwnyc.com
4. When they contact you just ask if they have recently serviced and if there are any alerts on their dashboard. If they have recently been serviced, the email doesn’t apply to them. It's just a generic email. If there are no alerts on the dashboard within 60 days of lease return they also do not need to come in.
