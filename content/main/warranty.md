## BMW/MINI Factory Warranties

Coverage carries to all BMW/MINI Service Centers

All maintenance plans are “condition based” which is defined as every 8-10k miles, 1 time per year, or if their vehicle is telling them service is needed. Those are the only times a customer will be covered by maintenance plan. This protects us from the OCD customer who wants an oil change every month and kills any chance of profit.

Once every twelve months. This means if the service was performed in January, it is not do until the next January unless the service indicator comes on. **Please note, MINI’s do not have a break in service.**

- Does not cover diagnostic work.
- You can see expiration dates/miles by visiting warranty vehicle inquiry on DCS S-gate. Also Roadside Assistance tow is covered while in active warranty.
- Maintenance plan is “condition based” meaning they can only use in 3 cases:
    1. Every 8-10k miles
    2. Once per year, or btw 10-12 months

Covers:

- Oil change
- Engine oil filter
- Brake Fluid
- Cabin microfilters
- Engine air filter

- Remote control/key battery

### Commonly Asked Questions

- Is a windshield covered?
    - No it is not. Any cracked windshield is at cost of the customer.
    - BMW/MINI’s standard policy is that customer pays for windshield up front and they submit bill to insurance company for reimbursement.
    - This is because a lot of insurance companies don’t pay for windshields.
    - Windshield related- Should I got to Safelite you are so expensive.
    - No, if this vehicle is a lease then you are required as part of your contract to replace with OEM parts otherwise at end of lease they will make you pay for a new windshield.
    - In addition, safelite offers a standard windshield that may not fit a BMW/MINI properly allowing leaks and damages. Also if the original windshield had a projection display safelite doesn’t offere that option. BMW/MINI windshield replacement takes 2-3 business days
