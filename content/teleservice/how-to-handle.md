## How to Handle Teleservice

1. Log into Teleservice
2. Go for the newest leads first. Click on “Days old” This will bring up the newest leads.
3. Select the customer through the empty circle icon and click on “service data” and “Service appointment”
    - **Service Data:** Will show what services that they need. **ONLY INDICATE WHAT IS CHECK MARKED**
    - **Service Appointment:** Shows calendar for call back, scheduled appointment, and notes
4. Open up the customers file on Mykaarma. I find that searching through the VIN number that is provided in service data gets the most accurate file.
5. Call the customer twice.

### If the customer picks up

**Script:** This is Lauren with the BMW Scheduling team. Your car alerted us that it is due for service (list the services). If this alert was in error or you have already serviced, please let us know.

**If the customer agrees to schedule:** Schedule them on MyKaarma then go to the “service appointment tab” and indicate the date of the appointment on the calendar and select AM or PM depending on the time of the appointment. Make sure to add the appointment details on the “Comments section”

### If the customer does not pick up

- Leave a Voicemail with the customer
- Send out a text and an email VIA Mykaarma
- Update this information on the notes and schedule a callback for the following day.

<span class="red">*If the customer does not want service at all cancel but if they do not answer you should keep the lead there*</span>
