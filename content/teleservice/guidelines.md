## Teleservice Guidelines

If a customer pushes back about how we should know if it is due, just respond that the “system is based on a program that works on automatic touch points and can sometimes send a signal in error, so to minimize your inconvenience we wanted to confirm.”

If an alert comes up only for standard scope (Multipoint inspection), just cancel the lead. Standard scope is only done in conjunction with other services. (Mini Diagnostic)

The touch points the car bases teleservice on is a yearly basis based on production date not retail date, so if a customer says I just bought the car 3 months ago and it shouldn’t need service you can explain the car was actually produced a year before. The alert also comes on every 8-10k miles.

Some customers get alarmed and demand immediate service with a loaner which we may not have, or some want to push it back a month or two. In either of those cases you can tell the customer we recommend servicing the vehicle within 1 month of the alert.
